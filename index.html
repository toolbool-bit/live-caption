Luxe Cam

Live subtitles, instant translation, and a penthouse desk behind you.

Press start and allow camera + mic.

Use it on a stranger site: open this page in Chrome, then in OBS add a Window Capture of this window (use Clean view and crop to the picture), press Start Virtual Camera, and choose “OBS Virtual Camera” as your camera on the chat site. Your voice is captioned live, so they read you in their language.
Speech recognition and translation run in Chrome or Edge on desktop. The translation language pack downloads once. There’s no AI cut-out of you here, so sit centered with a plain wall behind you and the soft frame blends you into the room.
